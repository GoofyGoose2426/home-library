# Phase 2, step 6: author pages and representation

Approved by Alexandra · 2026-10-30 (value lists as proposed; first batch of 10 read authors).

## What you'll get

- **An author page** for each of your 571 authors and narrators: their books in your library (read, unread, formats, series), and what they've said publicly about who they are, with the source link and their own words.
- **Author representation**, filled in by me in our sessions (no extra cost). I search for the author's **own** statements: their website bio, interviews, essays and own-voices notes. A value is saved only with a link to that page and a short quote. If I can't find the author saying it themselves, the value stays **unknown**. I never guess from a name, photo, nationality or the books they write.
- **Character representation** on each book page, suggested only from publisher descriptions or community representation lists (for example, a publisher's page or a "queer SFF" list), with the link and the line that says it.
- **Own voices**, suggested when the author and the main character share an identity that both have a source for. You confirm it.
- Everything arrives **unconfirmed**, in **To check**, under two new groups: "Author identity" and "Character representation". You can confirm, edit or reject it there or on the author page. Your edits always win.
- **New filters** in your library: each author category (for example, "author is 2SLGBTQIA+"), character representation and own voices.
- Viewers of your share link see author pages and representation too, as you decided. Only you can edit.

## The six author categories

For gender, 2SLGBTQIA+ and neurodivergence, a value is picked from a short list so filters and future stats can group them. The author's exact words are always kept in the quote. For race and ethnicity, nationality and region, and disability, the value is written in the author's own words, because a fixed list would put words in their mouth.

| Category | Values |
|---|---|
| Gender identity | Woman · Man · Non-binary · Genderfluid · Agender · Two-Spirit · Other (their words) |
| 2SLGBTQIA+ | Lesbian · Gay · Bisexual · Pansexual · Queer · Asexual · Aromantic · Trans · Intersex · Two-Spirit · Other (their words) |
| Race / ethnicity | Their own words (for example "Black British", "Mi'kmaw", "Korean American") |
| Disability | Their own words (for example "chronic illness", "Deaf", "wheelchair user") |
| Neurodivergence | Autistic · ADHD · Dyslexic · Dyspraxic · Other (their words) |
| Nationality / region | Their own words, as specific as they put it (for example "Atlantic Canadian", "Cree, Treaty 6") |

A category with no statement shows **unknown**. The author page also shows **when I last looked**, so you can tell "looked, found nothing public" apart from "not looked up yet".

## How each lookup works

1. Search the author's own website, publisher bio, interviews and essays.
2. Save only what the author says about themselves, with the page link and the line where they say it (one sentence at most).
3. If statements change over time (for example, a newer coming-out), use the most recent one.
4. Pen names shared by more than one writer, and authors with no public statements, stay unknown.
5. Character representation comes from the book's publisher description or a community representation list, never from my own reading of the plot.
